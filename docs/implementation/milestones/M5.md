## Limits

This is public synthetic software evidence, not biological validity, clinical
utility or real-study performance; no private study data were read or fitted.
The fixture requests offline execution, one worker, one CPU and 2 GiB; its
nested evaluation allows 20 fits within the execution limit of 40. These are
configured limits, not measured resource use. No permutation result is claimed
by the acceptance receipt. DIABLO, continuous block.spls and MOFA2 were not
registered at `85922cf`; the G5 release permitted subsequent packets to start,
not their acceptance.
