## Interfaces at the reviewed snapshot

- A-T02 exports `simulate_study()` and provides stage-A synthetic ingest. The
  implementation retains source-row feature identity, sample order, original
  missingness and structural absence; it refuses summary/statistic columns as
  measurements. Seed and planted truth are recorded separately.
- A-T03 supplies `prepare_fit(study, preparation_spec)` and
  `prepare_apply(study, preparation_spec, state_refs)` as **internal functions**
  at `8b0754c` and `e691ab9`. They use keyed study objects and immutable fit

  The [A-T03 registration request](../registrations/A-T03.json) requested their
  export, but neither snapshot's `NAMESPACE` exports them.

  observations and explicit links. Contradictory sample identity is refused;
  disabled clinical input reads no clinical source. The synthetic stage-A
  fixture also produced clinical payloads and envelopes, including a public
  view without the raw subject identifier.

## Limits

These are public synthetic fixtures, not real-study or biological validation.
The A-T09 example requests offline execution, one worker, one CPU and 2 GiB; its
configuration is not a measurement of resource use. Clinical objectives are
not fitted at stage A. Local real-data artifacts are sensitive, and
`approved_aggregate` remains unavailable without an approved disclosure policy
at this snapshot. Acceptance did not establish G3/G4 or validate biomarkers,
preprocessing optimality or clinical associations.
