existing ticket index and a representative ticket, A/B/C/D specifications and

## No hidden freeze claim

The files are checksum-locked so the independent audit sees a precise candidate.
They are not labelled as independently approved or runtime-validated. G0 transitions
only after actual audit and correction evidence. A specification may correctly
contain bounded conditional methods; those methods cannot be called implementation-
ready or done until their distinct method/rights/runtime gates pass. No claim that
all 41 tickets are currently runnable is made.

## User-specific decisions that cannot be invented

Real field meanings/units/visit links, target exposure context, desired contrasts/
adjustments and effect units, external resource entitlements, institution-specific
clinical disclosure threshold and a particular clinical prediction endpoint are
not inferable from the four planning files. They are required inputs to relevant
real analyses, not excuses to reopen the settled architecture. Unaffected synthetic
implementation can proceed after G0. No new questionnaire is necessary for choices
already recorded in the repository.
