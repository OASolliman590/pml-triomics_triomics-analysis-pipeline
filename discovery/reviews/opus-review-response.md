## Corrections to the raw review

1. **Organophosphate:** the audit records candidate quantitative columns in mixed contrast sheets. Opus's table says no sample-level matrix was observed and assigns every sheet to results/lists. That is too strong: candidate measurements exist, although no complete scientifically usable matrix has been validated. Corrected in the project proposal.
2. **Prevalence:** per-feature observation prevalence divides by eligible samples, not feature inventory. Selection changes the feature population described; unknown original observation masks separately limit original-prevalence claims.
3. **Selection/inference:** an exported complete table does not prove validity; a subset is not universally invalid. Justified targeted families and suitable independent filters may support valid testing under their conditions. Require selection/family evidence, not a universe-complete boolean alone. Unknown/outcome-selected data have no ordinary BH claim without an approved valid method. Withholding q does not repair selection bias in effects/SE/p. Root checked [Bourgon et al., 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2906865/) for filter/test validity conditions; no new selection-aware method is adopted.
4. **Evaluation:** externally fixed panels can be eligible for independent evaluation with documented construction. Selection using held-out outcomes or unknown leakage cannot. Learned preparation/selection remains within appropriate folds. Root checked [Ambroise and McLachlan, 2002](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC124442/) on validating the selection procedure.
5. **Aliases:** measurement granularity and identification specificity are different concepts. Mapping ambiguity is not aggregation membership. Shared aliases alone neither verify identity nor authorize collapsing/summing measured rows.
6. **Maps:** whitespace deletion alone is not invertible; preserving original and canonical values makes mapping history auditable. Mechanically applying an authorized map is not independently deciding specimen identity. The same approved map needs no repeated confirmation.
7. **Group-by-visit:** retained-signal orthogonality is necessary, not proof of covariance/rank/df validity or protected common-row compatibility. Difference-in-change stays conditional. Between-group targets remain visible pending suitable representation, not silently removed from scope.
8. **Attrition:** per-group counts and target-population limitations are mandatory. Equal counts do not establish ignorable missingness; inequality alone does not force partial status or invalidate inference. No arbitrary balance rule or universal ban on future reviewed missing-data methods is adopted.
9. **Prior review:** Opus did not read the individual historical reviews. A-spec-review.md and the A/D re-review record root review of agent-authored A; the self-review caveat applied to the separate cross-component review. Thus absence of independent A review was incorrectly inferred. Those historical reviews do not approve this amendment or close G0.
10. **Conversion:** declarations and hashes alone cannot establish faithful extraction. Actual re-derivation/comparison evidence and later implementation verification are required. No workbook conversion is implemented or certified here.
