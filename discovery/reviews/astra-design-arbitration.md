## Accepted direction

**1. One representation per Analysis/context.** Freeze the molecular values, feature/unit maps and `representation_id` per `(Study, Analysis, preparation_context)` for B and C. Two-occasion change and difference-in-change use signed subject deltas; a declared between-subject modifier represents an interaction with occasion in the original repeated model. Time-invariant covariates do not become within-person main effects. Levels and subject means use distinct mean-retaining Analyses. The unit map makes delta score rows subject-contrast units: this explicitly amends the original “samples × components” semantics. No double centering, projected-out target or covert B/C matrix substitution is allowed. Prediction requiring both visits is eligible only when both belong to the deployment information set.

For >2 visits, retain the centered-response/fixed-subject branch under a declared full design and residual model. Check estimability per feature; calculate residual df from actual fitted rank and the model’s weight/covariance treatment. The simple unweighted full-rank linear fit uses `n_obs - rank(X)`. The balanced shortcut is not general. Centering by subject constants preserves ordinary non-subject coefficients/residuals relative to the corresponding raw fixed-subject fit; it does not establish covariance adequacy or moderation validity. No production naive centering-plus-ordinary-duplicateCorrelation default. Use `trend=FALSE` initially; another moderation trend needs a specified covariate and validation.

Irregular visits are **not automatically invalid**. A continuous-time/spline design may qualify if time units, origin, basis/knots, contrasts, observation process, rank, within-subject support and residual assumptions are explicit, and reference agreement plus null/uncertainty calibration pass over its supported time/missingness patterns. Unsupported covariance, random slopes or an unidentified target remains unavailable pending a separately specified and validated branch. Preserve trajectory scope.

**2. Fit/apply and ownership.** Fit-mode profile operations emit typed provisional state through reserved SE metadata. A validates it, atomically writes a content-addressed `PreprocessingState`, and substitutes the immutable reference. Apply resolves it through `params` and cannot refit. State binds training units, eligible features, original observation-mask provenance, learned parameters/reference sets, schema/package/RNG versions and operation order. Non-inductive methods are exploratory-only for incompatible predictive tasks. All evaluation partitions are subject-disjoint, with temporal restrictions when the objective requires them; learned preprocessing and selection are nested inside training.

Adopt one versioned shared-schema module in the single R package, through existing A-T01/A-T02. A owns ingest/linkage, B owns every association fit, C owns score/prediction lineage, D owns evidence routing/rendering. The orchestrator schedules new B requests on immutable C/D endpoint artifacts; B does not invoke its producers. No separate schema package or parallel schema milestone.

**3. Multiplicity default.** Adopt BH per fixed `(Study, Analysis, Block, scalar_contrast, claim_scope)` family over its declared eligible features. Species/classes/derived features are not split opportunistically; a different partition needs a predeclared scientific claim. Multiple contrasts are separate by default. A joint multi-contrast claim explicitly fixes a joint within-Block family before testing. Candidate unions across families carry **no union-wide FDR claim**. Another Block cannot change existing q values. Another genuinely separate contrast creates another family; renaming analyses cannot erase selection or data reuse. Clinical endpoint and cross-block score/pathway families retain their distinct registered scopes.

For an otherwise valid fixed family, use tested p values and internal `p=1` for failed/non-estimable members; publish failed-row p/q as unavailable, their placeholder and complete disposition counts. BH claims require valid null p values and a justified dependence regime (for example independence or the relevant positive-dependence condition), plus valid selection/model assumptions. Replacing missing tests by 1 cannot repair invalid filtering, selected-source inference or an invalid model. No automatic claim under arbitrary dependence. With unresolved validity, withhold inferential q; retain descriptive evidence. Prevalence/coverage filtering requires a method-specific justification, not merely a timestamp before fitting.

**4. DIABLO common meaning.** Require explicit `score_definition=consensus_mean` for the initial common-result route; no implicit consensus default or reference-block fallback. The block design matrix remains explicitly configured as originally required. Top-level `scores` is mandatory. Native backend success without a valid common score/variance contract is an unavailable common result with diagnostics, never a successful object containing invented or NA-filled fields.

For each component, freeze the required views and eligible training-unit alignment set before fitting. Choose the anchor by lexicographic stable `block_key`, independent of input order. Orient it using the largest-absolute native loading, breaking exact ties by stable `feature_key`. Require finite nonzero anchor loadings. For each other block, estimate its correlation with the oriented anchor on the declared common training units; flip its scores and associated loading/weight direction together to make that correlation positive. Reject undefined or numerically zero correlation and zero/near-zero variate SD. Freeze numerical tolerances (default `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)` on dimensionless correlation and relative-scale degeneracy checks), print/hash them, and do not describe these as statistical evidence thresholds.

Store signs, training means/SDs, equal weights `1/N`, required views, alignment units and native prediction state. The consensus is the mean of the signed, training-standardized native variates. Apply native prediction and these frozen transformations to held-out units; never learn an orientation or scale on held-out data, and never assume `X %*% loadings` reconstructs a backend score. Common loadings are sign-oriented native loadings with their role/scale declared; native diagnostics preserve original coordinates. This is a score convention, not proof of a shared biological direction. Missing required views make that unit/component unavailable; do not average fewer views. A successful common object must cover its declared unit/component set with finite values; omissions require a previously declared eligibility/attrition policy, not silent adapter trimming.

**5. Variance and selection.** `variance` means backend-native block-by-component proportion explained for the native model, **not variance explained by consensus scores**. Each adapter must establish the actual native statistic, assay space, denominator, component correspondence and incremental/cumulative convention from the pinned implementation and numeric checks. Convert verified percentages to proportions; do not pass percentages through, convert arbitrary eigenvalues, clamp unsuitable statistics or invent a decomposition. Preserve exact native values/units separately. Sign changes do not alter an explained-variance metric, but scaling or coordinate changes do not make a different metric interchangeable. If native output is absent, undefined or incompatible, the common result stays unavailable until an explicitly specified reconstruction metric passes review and tests. No pooled consensus fraction or unsupported cross-backend comparison.

DIABLO `selected` is executed sparse support. Dense PCA/MOFA requires an explicit selection rule; `none` produces empty selections and no C nominations. A loading threshold is a configuration choice, never significance or causality. Native MOFA enrichment additionally needs its approved model/data/annotation sidecar.

**6. Remaining convergence adopted.** Use provenance-defined profiles and reversible typed feature/source/clinical identities; keep clinical values outside omics Blocks. Preserve authorized abundance quantities and derive lipid totals on the fixed parent universe before log/closure/centering. Compositional Analyses require explicit reference/zero policies. No universal correlation fallback, clinical-imputation substitution or silently inferred linkage. D receives full tested/failed/family and integration evidence, not candidate-only lists; both B/C nominations reach both namespace-compatible routes. Resource rights/version gates and confidentiality restrictions remain explicit. Synthetic recovery is assessed through exact invariants, prespecified strong-signal controls and repeated null/weak/adversarial distributions, never a guarantee for every planted factor.
