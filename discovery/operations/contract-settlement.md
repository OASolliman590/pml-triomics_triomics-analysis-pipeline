## Authoritative choices

1. **Analysis units and repeated inference.** B and C consume identical molecular values/maps within each Study/Analysis/preparation context. Signed subject deltas are primary for two-occasion change/difference-in-change, with complete-pair error by default and explicit attrition mode. Between-person levels/means use separate mean-retaining Analyses. For >2 visits, centered-response plus fixed-subject designs remain conditional on explicit residual assumptions, per-feature estimability/rank and calibrated uncertainty. Ordinary df uses actual model rank, not a universal balanced formula. No naive centered-response/ordinary duplicateCorrelation default. Irregular times may qualify under an explicit validated time/basis/covariance contract; trajectory scope is retained. Initial moderation trend is false; alternative trend/count-prior treatment requires a specified and tested reference.
2. **Immutable preprocessing state.** Profile signatures remain `(se, params) -> SE`. Canonical reserved fields are `params$mode` (`fit` or `apply`), `params$fitted_state` for resolved immutable apply state, and `metadata(se)$triomics$state_payload` for provisional fit output. The outer A service validates/externalizes the payload and replaces it with `metadata(se)$triomics$state_ref`. State binds training identities, original mask, feature set, learned references/parameters, method/version/RNG and operation order. Apply cannot refit. Non-inductive methods are unavailable for incompatible prediction contexts. Subject-disjoint/temporally valid splits and all learned selection stay inside training.
3. **Shared schema and ownership.** One versioned schema module inside triomics owns source/feature/subject/sample/unit maps, preprocessing state, clinical dictionaries/observations/links, objectives, endpoint artifacts and result envelopes. A-T01/02 introduce it through runnable validation/ingest. A owns preparation/linkage; B owns all statistical and clinical fits; C owns integration/held-out scoring/evaluation; D owns annotation/rendering. A later C/D score may trigger a new B request through the orchestrator; B never invokes its producers. Both B/C nominations reach both namespace-compatible D routes with full eligible evidence.
4. **Multiplicity.** Default BH families are fixed per `(Study, Analysis, Block, scalar_contrast, claim_scope)`. Multiple contrasts have separate predeclared families unless a joint claim declares a joint within-Block family. No union-wide FDR claim across separate families. Failed/non-estimable members use internal p=1; their published p/q remain unavailable, with placeholder and complete member counts. Valid null p values, dependence/selection/model assumptions and justified filtering are prerequisites; unresolved validity withholds inferential q. Clinical endpoint and cross-block score/pathway families stay separately registered. Adding a Block cannot alter another Block's q.
5. **DIABLO.** Require explicit `score_definition=consensus_mean` and the original explicitly configured block design matrix. Use the exact training anchor/sign/scaling/tolerance algorithm in Astra sections 4–5; freeze required views and training units, apply native prediction plus stored transforms, and retain native diagnostics. Do not independently orient blocks by their own arbitrary sign conventions or infer prediction from loading multiplication. Undefined correlation/degenerate SD or missing required output makes the common result unavailable under the declared attrition policy. Consensus is a convention, not native prediction or a biological effect.
6. **Variance/selection/NA.** Common variance is a verified native block/component proportion with assay space, denominator and incremental/cumulative semantics. Percentages are converted only with verified units; missing/incompatible output cannot become a successful NA-filled object. Reconstruction metrics need separate reviewed definitions. Sparse selection is executed nonzero support; dense PCA/MOFA require an explicit rule, including `none` producing no C nominations. Release-pinned fit/tune/predict NA capability cells open only after executable checks; no hidden imputation/zero-fill or row deletion.
7. **Profiles and derived quantities.** Behavior follows acquisition/quantity/scale/missingness provenance, not names. Unknown mechanism cannot authorize a mechanism-specific imputer. Preserve authorized abundance quantities, derive lipid totals from a fixed parent universe before log/closure/centering, and distinguish partial/estimated/unavailable endpoints. Compositional Analyses require explicit reference/zero policies, defaulting to refusal of unresolved zeros. Named preprocessing method/configuration is required where no universally defensible default exists; this is a settled configuration rule, not permission to silently choose one. Every enabled method must meet its API and predictive-apply contract.
8. **Clinical/target/resource interpretation.** No inferred clinical crosswalk/code/units or universal correlation fallback. Keep multi-response sets, censoring qualifiers, static conflicts and visit exceptions explicit. Scores retain training/outcome/selection lineage. Target evidence records overlap/dependence and never turns association into binding. Resources require exact release/hash/rights/operation records; confidential hosted upload exclusion is a project restriction, not an uncited assertion about all vendors. Required-unavailable and optional-unavailable outputs differ; neither establishes biological absence or completes a conditional adapter ticket.
9. **CLI and milestone boundaries.** Adopt the [command contract](implementation-command-contract.md): `--validate-only`, stages A/B/C/D/report/all and a single output root. A-T01 owns the dependency-free native build/check wrapper. B-T07 produces Markdown reproduction; report and final QMD vignettes remain milestones 8 and 9. Existing 41 [ticket cards](../tickets/README.md) preserve IDs and add explicit clinical handoff edges; no extra parallel schema milestone.

## Shared service interfaces to implement

These names and arguments freeze the internal cross-module seams in the single package; they are specification contracts, not existing R functions. The CLI is the public orchestration surface. They do not add a seventh BlockProfile method or change the protected simulator/IntegrationResult fields.

| Owner/seam | Inputs | Output and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| A `prepare_fit(study, preparation_spec)` | Validated Study, resolved profiles, declared training units/context and source/mask hashes | PreparedStudy artifact plus validated state refs; output contains processed values and registries, not a B model |
| A `prepare_apply(study, preparation_spec, state_refs)` | New eligible units, same resolved method contract, immutable training-state refs | PreparedStudy in apply context; no training/outcome refit |
| B `resolve_analysis(prepared_study, analysis_spec)` | PreparedStudy, estimand/design/representation specification | ResolvedAnalysis with keyed matrices, AnalysisUnitMap, model/contrast plan, retained-signal and eligibility diagnostics |
| B `fit_block_models(resolved_analysis, statistics_spec)` | Immutable common matrices, registered hypotheses and explicit inference/sensitivity policy | Full tested/failed B result bundle with family and candidate records |
| C `integrate_blocks(resolved_analysis, integration_spec)` | Same context matrices, explicit backend parameters and fit/evaluation plan | Protected IntegrationResult only on common-contract success; separate status/diagnostic/state artifacts on all outcomes |
| B `associate_endpoints(endpoint_bundle, clinical_view, objective_spec)` | Immutable feature/score endpoints, A-generated clinical view and resolved registered objective/family | ClinicalAssociationResult bundle, diagnostics/failed members and family ledger; no C/D callback |
| D `annotate_evidence(evidence_bundle, annotation_spec)` | Full B/C evidence, independent nominations, identities and approved resource/input contracts | Mapping/enrichment/target evidence plus route statuses; no upstream refitting |
| D `render_report(report_bundle, report_spec)` | Immutable A/B/C/D and clinical export artifacts | HTML/export manifest and rendering status separate from analysis status |

Artifact envelopes require `schema_version`, `artifact_type`, `artifact_id`, `study_id`, `analysis_id` where applicable, `preparation_context_id` where applicable, source/input hashes, resolved configuration hash and status. Unknown schema versions or stale hashes fail before numerical dispatch. Stable feature/sample/unit keys remain reversible through shared registries. Native backend objects cross no seam except explicitly typed version-bound state/sidecars; D does not inspect them ad hoc.

## Decision and gate dispositions

DEC-02–12 and CL-ADR01–04 now have the bounded design choices above; protected semantic changes are recorded in [ADR 0001](../../docs/adr/0001-analysis-context-and-shared-contracts.md). Exact numerical/user-required parameters remain explicit configuration, including contrasts/objectives, q/effect thresholds, imputation/reference policies, DIABLO design/tuning and MOFA factors/convergence. Choosing a numeric value for a real study without its scientific question is not engineering settlement.

Empirical gates remain unrun: reference ordinary/moderated inference, repeated/time-model calibration and per-feature rank; fold-local mutation invariance; NA and DIABLO native-to-common/prediction/variance checks; family accounting and filtering/dependence validity; DEqMS on chosen representation; MOFA training/reload/stability; lipid mass-balance and derived-parent coverage. Each enabled capability must pass its gate; deferred capabilities remain explicitly incomplete.
